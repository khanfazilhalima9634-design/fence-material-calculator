About Fence Material Calculator

This free fence calculator helps homeowners estimate the number of fence posts, rails, pickets, concrete bags and total material costs for wood privacy fences.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are fence posts calculated?

Posts = Ceiling(Fence Length ÷ Post Spacing) + 1.

How many concrete bags per post?

This calculator estimates two 80-lb concrete bags per post.

Does this include waste?

Yes. A waste percentage is added to the picket calculation.
